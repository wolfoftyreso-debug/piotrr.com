# Piotrr — Vision

## What we are building

Piotrr is a B2B and B2C marketplace connecting customers with **verified
companies** across Europe. The long-term ambition is to become the **Alibaba of
European services** — Europe's most trusted marketplace for professional services —
starting with construction and automotive repair.

We win by combining:

- Verified businesses
- Transparent reputation
- Standardized company profiles
- Efficient quotation workflows
- Integrated logistics
- Secure transactions
- Scalable marketplace architecture
- Strong trust mechanisms

## Marketplace principle

**Piotrr never performs the work.** The platform connects:

```
Customers
   ↓
Verified companies
   ↓
Payment
   ↓
Logistics
   ↓
Reviews
   ↓
Long-term trust
```

## Phase 1 — Initial marketplaces

### 1. Construction Marketplace

Companies create a standardized business profile — think:

> LinkedIn Company Page + Facebook Business Page + Alibaba Supplier + Trustpilot +
> Google Business, combined into one professional profile.

Every company page is SEO-friendly and has its own **permanent URL**, e.g.
`piotrr.com/company/buildpro-estonia`.

Customers post Requests For Quote (RFQ) for jobs such as house renovation, roof
replacement, plumbing, electrical work, painting, flooring and carpentry. Companies
receive RFQs matching their services and respond with offers.

### 2. Automotive Marketplace

Customers request quotes for vehicle services: paint work, body/collision repair,
dent removal, rust repair, smart repair, wheel refurbishment, detailing, ceramic
coating, wrapping, PPF, windshield replacement, mechanical repairs, diagnostics,
service, engine rebuild, transmission work.

Customers upload photos, VIN, registration number, damage description, optional
insurance information and a desired completion date. Workshops submit quotations.

## Trust as the core product

Trust is what differentiates Piotrr from classified-ads sites:

- **Company Trust Score** — automatically and continuously calculated from verified
  identity, verified VAT, company age, completed projects, reviews, ratings,
  response time/rate, dispute history, documentation quality and verification level.
- **Verification levels** — Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum. Each level is
  increasingly difficult to obtain and grants higher visibility in search.
- **Verified reviews** — only verified customers may review, and only after
  completed work. Reviews cover overall rating, communication, quality, price,
  delivery, professionalism, would-recommend, photos and a project description.

## Logistics

Many repairs require transportation. The platform supports logistics providers
(vehicle transport, freight, couriers) with door-to-door transport, pickup booking,
tracking, delivery confirmation, transport insurance, estimated delivery time and
shipping labels. The logistics module is **independent from the marketplace** so it
can serve any vertical — or operate standalone.

## Future expansion

The marketplace engine stays generic; each vertical defines its own categories,
metadata, workflows and filters. Planned candidate verticals:

Manufacturing · Industrial suppliers · Agriculture · Logistics · Legal services ·
Accounting · Healthcare · Consultants · Freelancers · Property services · Cleaning ·
Security · Renewable energy · Installation services

Adding a vertical must never require changing the core architecture.
